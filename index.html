Overview

An AI companion for writing — one that helps you find the writer already living inside you. The bet is simple: help you write better, so that you keep writing at all.

It started from a personal problem. I write in notebooks and never look back, because reading myself is uncomfortable — the inner critic is always the cruelest one. yoescritor is meant to sit next to that voice rather than replace it: closer to a note in the margin than to a chat window.
